Who we are

About Sree Mithra

A specialist clinic in Tanuku for dental, implant and cosmetic care, and for the treatment of facial trauma and maxillofacial conditions.

Because our team covers both dentistry and surgery of the jaw and face, many patients can be assessed and treated in one place, with each step explained before it begins.

  • Specialist dental careCheck-ups, fillings, root canals and extractions.
  • Implants and surgeryMissing teeth replaced; surgical extractions.
  • Facial and jaw careInjuries, jaw joint problems and facial surgery.
  • Patient-centred approachUnhurried consultations, clear explanations.
