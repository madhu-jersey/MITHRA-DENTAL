Maxillofacial

Common problems

Common problems

Signs that it may be time to see a specialist.

Jaw joint pain or clicking

Pain, clicking or locking near the ear when opening or closing the mouth.

Facial swelling

Swelling of the face or jaw, sometimes with pain or fever.

Cysts

Fluid-filled swellings that can form in the jaw.

Wisdom tooth surgery

Wisdom teeth that are stuck or growing at an angle.

Oral lesions

Patches, lumps or ulcers in the mouth that do not heal.

Jaw misalignment

Upper and lower jaws that do not meet properly.

Treatment

Solutions and treatment options

The right option depends on your examination. Recovery times are confirmed by the doctor for each patient.

Surgical extraction

What it involves
Removing teeth that cannot be taken out simply, such as impacted wisdom teeth.
Who it suits
Impacted, broken or hard-to-reach teeth.
Recovery time
To be confirmed by the doctor after assessment

Cyst removal

What it involves
Surgically removing a cyst from the jaw and checking the area as it heals.
Who it suits
Cysts found on examination or imaging.
Recovery time
To be confirmed by the doctor after assessment

Jaw joint treatment

What it involves
Assessing the joint and recommending treatment for pain, clicking or locking.
Who it suits
Ongoing jaw joint pain or restricted opening.
Recovery time
To be confirmed by the doctor after assessment

Biopsy and lesion removal

What it involves
Removing a lesion, or a small sample of it, to be examined.
Who it suits
Lumps, patches or ulcers in the mouth that do not heal.
Recovery time
To be confirmed by the doctor after assessment

Orthognathic (jaw) surgery referral

What it involves
Assessing jaw position and referring for corrective jaw surgery where needed.
Who it suits
Significant jaw misalignment affecting bite or appearance.
Recovery time
To be confirmed by the doctor after assessment
